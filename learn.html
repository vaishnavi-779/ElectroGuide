Learn Electronics

Follow a practical path from circuit fundamentals to embedded systems and communication.

Components

Resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, LEDs, transistors and ICs.

03

Rectifiers, amplifiers, op-amps, filters, oscillators and transistor circuits.

04

Digital Electronics

Logic gates, Boolean algebra, flip-flops, counters, registers, ADC and DAC.

05

Microcontrollers

GPIO, ADC, PWM, timers, interrupts, UART, SPI, I²C and board architecture.

06

Sensors, drivers, firmware structure, real-time thinking and hardware interfaces.

07

Sampling, modulation, bandwidth, antennas and digital communication basics.

08

Apply what you know to IoT, robotics, automation, sensing and communication projects.
